Inspiration: Letterbox, Metacritic, Serializd, BookVillage

# Analysis of common features/functionality of Metacritic/Letterbox/Serialized/Book Village

 - There is a rating system on all four sites, and ratings can be changed based on comments.

- Promotion is based on ratings on certain sites, such as Metacritic, while others, such as Serialized, do not display ratings; you have to click on the movie/series to get more information.

- On Serealizd and Letterbox, ratings are also displayed based on the current moment.
You can see that a movie or series was rated 2 stars a month ago but has rise to 4 stars,
while on Metacritic you can only see the current rating.

- Serealizd works with images only to better attract the eye, unlike others that use much more text.

- bookmark displays a lot of information compared to others, but it does not have a rating or an indication of the amount of CO2 per liter of water used for your order.

# Specific analysis of Serealizd

- The navigation method is vertical regardless of the site.

- Serealizd uses a lot of images to attract the eye and very few buttons to make navigation very simple.

- Their statistics are based on the year, so you can see the success of the series as the year progresses.

- They have a simple tag system with an emoji in each tag to make it easier to understand the categories.

- They have different categories below the series once selected.
On the home page, you can see the seasons, in reviews you can see the comments, in lists you can see categories such as series for streaming, in media you can see posters, banners, and videos about the series, in Cast you can see the actors, and in similar shows you can find series of the same type.

- You can see on which platform you can stream the series.

- You can see when the next episode will be released, as well as the first and last broadcast dates.

- They also advertise their social media accounts at the bottom of the page, as well as their app.

- In Discover, you can also view series by category.

- Serealizd has a pretty nice tag system that clearly explains the category of the movie/series.

- On smartphones, you have to create an account to access the site's content.

# Specific analysis of Letterboxd

- Uses a very simple background to highlight the content in the middle.

- At the top of the page, you can read the journal and see the actors/directors in the “Members” section and how many films they have appeared in.

- In the members section, there is a list of users with their favorite films, how many films they have liked, and their playlists.

- In the middle of the page, there is a list of films. When you hover your mouse over them, you can see the number of views and the number of likes.

- A little further down, you can see users' comments on the films with their ratings.

- There is a note in the middle of the page explaining that you can keep track of every movie you've ever watched, create lists, and share your opinions on movies with a rating from 1 to 5 and a comment. You can also compile and share comments and follow your friends to read theirs.

- When you click on a movie, you can see statistics on the movie's rating, lots of comments on the movie, information about the studio, the language, the languages into which the movie has been translated, and the countries where it is available.
The director and the entire team behind the film's creation.

- At the bottom of the page, you will find contact information, help, and advertising for their social networks.

- At the bottom of the page, you will also see advertising on the left and at the very bottom.

- On smartphones, you have to create an account to access the site's content.

# Specific analysis of Metacritic

- The page has bright colors that really make the series stand out. In each box, there is a score out of 100 indicating the popularity of the film, without showing whether its score has increased over time or not.
The score is also characterized by colors ranging from red to orange, taking up a large portion of the screen.

- You can scroll horizontally through the movies/series to see if there are others that interest you.

- A little further down, you can also see information about video games.

- When you click on a movie/series, you will see a presentation video and its rating on the right.
If you scroll down a little further, you will see a list of all the episodes. Click on them to see their ratings/comments.

- At the bottom of the page, once you have selected the series/movie, you will see all the comments with their rating out of 100, prominently displayed in large font.

- At the very bottom of the page, you will see information related to what you have loaded, as well as additional information such as support, digital services, privacy policy, and advertising for their social networks.

- Whether you click on a game/movie/series, it always works the same way with a rating out of 100 and comments above it.

- At the top of the page, you can also see that you can choose categories such as games, movies, music, TV series, and news, with a rather large search button on the right and a button to register to its right.

- The fact that the background is white makes the other colors of the buttons stand out better.

# Specific analysis of Book Village

- The site is white in color, which makes the books and text stand out.

- On the page, you can see several books categorized by condition, best sellers, and others. You can also see the top 50 authors that are apparently the most popular.

- At the bottom of the page, there are articles, a help center, prices, contact information, and an advertisement for their app.

- At the top of the page, there is a large image advertising their app.

- The page contains a lot of text in a rather small font.

- At the top of the page, there are categories such as comics and food, novels/literature, book series, education, science and technology, culture and religion, and practical arts.

- The buttons for creating/logging into an account are small and located on the right, not very prominent, unlike the book sales button to their right.

- There is a button at the top right of the page to change the page theme to dark.

- Above the books, you can see the user selling them and below them their price, the author, and the title of the book.

- When you click on a book, you can see a summary of the book, the book cover on the left, the genre of the book, the barcode, the publication date of the book, the number of pages, the height, width, weight, and thickness.
On the right, you can see the payment methods and the CO2/water cost of the order.
At the bottom of this page, you can see advertisements for other books by the same author/in the same literary genre.
